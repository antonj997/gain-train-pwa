## 17 November 2026 — sort by hold and drag

- **Reorder exercises and Reorder sets: hold a row and drag it.** The ↑ and ↓ buttons are gone.
  - Hold a row for 250 ms, or press its grip for at once, and it **lifts**: a little larger (spring `SPRING.lift`), on a raised surface with a shadow, and a short vibration where the phone has one.
  - It follows the finger while the rows it passes **slide out of its way**, leaving a gap where it will land.
  - Let go and it **settles into the gap** (`SPRING.drop`).
  - A finger that moves before the hold still scrolls the list, and a downward swipe still closes the sheet. Held at the sheet's edge, the list scrolls.
  - With a mouse, dragging a row picks it up.
- **Commit once, at release.** The DOM order stays put while a row is held. On release the new order is committed at once, and the dropped row springs into its slot from where the finger left it (FLIP), so the next row can be picked up straight away.
  - Escape, or dropping it where it was, sends it back while the others slide home.
  - Reduced motion keeps the lift state but drops the movement.
- **Keyboard.** The grip still moves a row: Space or Enter, the arrow keys, Escape. The screen-reader instructions are now translated.
- `src/lib/sort-drag.ts` holds the slot geometry (`slotPitch`, `slotShift`, `dropOffset`, `moveItem`), tested in `tests/sort-drag.test.ts`. The old fake-hook harness for SortableSetList (`tests/set-component.test.ts`) drove the removed pointer handlers; a Playwright smoke now covers that behaviour in Chromium and WebKit:

  - edge scroll, the keyboard, and Reorder sets.
  `moveId` and the arrow strings are removed.

## 16 November 2026 — lift goals measure strength, with check-ins

- **Why.** A goal of Bench Press 110 kg × 1 showed “now 100 kg, 0 %, Behind, −1.1 kg/week” for someone who had gone from 100 × 2 to 90 × 7–6, about 111 kg estimated: stronger, not behind. A one-rep goal tracked the heaviest weight, and the pace was a least-squares slope through six weeks of those weights.
- **Strength, not weight.** A lift goal's progress reads the estimated one-rep max of each session's best set of 1–10 reps (Epley as in `estimate.ts`, the whole load for bodyweight lifts), whatever the goal's reps. A single is its own load: 110 × 1 is 110 kg of strength, not 113.7 (Epley at one rep), and the target is measured the same way, so 100 × 5 is ≈ 116.7. Now reads “≈ 111 kg uppsk.” with the set and day under it (“90 × 7, 13 nov.”), the best session of the last four weeks; Start and Target sit on a line under it. The bar runs from the start's strength to the target's and stops at 99 % until the goal is met.
- **Smoothed status.** The noisy slope chip (“Efter schemat”) is gone for lift goals. The chip compares the mean per-session strength of the last four weeks with the four before: Framåt (more than 2.5 % up), Stilla (within ±2.5 %), Bakåt (more than 2.5 % down), or För lite data (under three sessions in a window). With enough data the trend is carried on to the date: “I fas mot datumet” or “Inte i fas mot datumet än”; “I den här takten är du där runt <date>” stays only while the trend goes up. Bodyweight goals keep their pace as before.
- **Reached by the real lift.** A goal of W × R is met only by a checked working set of at least W for at least R reps (bodyweight lifts: the added weight). When the smoothed strength gets there first the card says “Redo att testa: försök 110 × 1”. Reached goals, `reachedOn` and the Reached list are unchanged; there is no celebration to keep.
- **Check-ins (kontrollpass).** Four weeks after the goal was made or the last check-in, or on the day of the sixth session with the lift since, whichever is first, the goal is due a check-in for that Monday-to-Sunday week: as many reps as possible at 85 % of the target on the lift's load step (`loadIncrement`/`roundToStep`; 5 lb in pounds), “Kontrollpass den här veckan: 92,5 kg × max”. A plan's deload week starting within the four weeks puts it in the week before. A set at that weight or heavier during the week does it, and the card then shows “Senaste kontroll: ≈ 108 kg, <date>”. Skip (“Hoppa över”) moves the next one an interval on; so does a week that passes. A goal that is ready to test has no check-in. Due weeks and results are worked out from the log; only skipped weeks are stored, as an optional `checkinSkips` list on the goal record, so a skip syncs to the other devices. The server trigger does not check unknown goal fields, so no migration is needed.
- **In the workout.** An exercise with an open lift goal has a small goal mark by its name (target icon and “110 × 1”; only the icon under 360 px; spoken “Mål: Bench Press 110 kg × 1”; it opens nothing). In a check-in week the first working set's target under Förra reads “Kontroll 92,5 × max” (a tap fills the weight, the reps are left to type; the Set sheet says “92,5 kg × max”), and a goal that is ready to test puts “Testa 110 × 1” on the top set.
- **Everywhere.** The Goals page, Progress's goal strip (“Redo att testa”, “Kontrollpass”, or “Bakåt” in warm) and the plan status page's goals use the same model. The goal editor explains the measure with a new line and keeps skipped weeks when a goal is edited.
- Pure helpers in `src/data/lift-goal.ts` and `src/data/goal-targets.ts`, tested in `tests/lift-goal.test.ts` (series and windows, the 2.5 % edges, too little data, check-in due dates by four weeks, six sessions, a deload and a skip, the check-in weight, the real-lift rule with bodyweight, ready to test, and the owner's case); the goal tests that assumed the weight mode now check strength.

## 16 November 2026 — the live workout on a touch screen: the typed field, locked sets, fast pad taps

- **The field the pad types** stands out from the rest of its row: a filled accent tone, a 3 px accent ring and bolder digits, with a thin line under the digits that breathes like a caret (it holds still with reduced motion). The row's other fields step back (no accent outline, quieter digits). Light and dark; nothing in the row moves.
- **Checked sets are locked** in a live workout: their fields never show a focus or pad ring, take no focus from a tap or Tab, and the badge's Set sheet, Previous, drag-to-step and the pad wait until the set is unchecked. One tap on a checked row says nothing; a second within 2 s shows “Uncheck this set to edit its values.” (`lockedTap` in `src/lib/locked-tap.ts`; a keyboard is told at once). The ✓ unchecks as before. Editing a finished workout is unchanged.
- **Swiping away a checked set** asks first, once per device: the row slides fully open in its place with “Delete a checked set?” / “Ta bort avbockat set?”, “Shown only once.”, Cancel and Delete. Delete plays the bubble as before; Cancel or a tap elsewhere slides it back. Either answer is kept on the device (`gt-checked-delete-asked`, `src/lib/checked-delete.ts`). Unchecked sets delete as before.
- **Keep screen on** / **Håll skärmen igång**: a switch in the running workout's details sheet (tap the workout's name), on by default, shown only where the browser has the Screen Wake Lock API. Off releases the lock at once and on asks again; the choice is kept on the device (`gt-keep-screen-on`, `src/lib/keep-screen-on.ts`, `useKeepScreenOn`).
- **From the Set sheet to a field in one tap.** With a set's sheet open, a tap on any set's load or reps closes the sheet and opens the pad on that field; a tap anywhere else only closes it. The page under Radix's backdrop takes no pointer events, so the field is found by the fields' boxes (`padFieldAt` in `src/lib/number-pad.ts`, `components/workout/pad-hit.ts`).
- **Fast pad taps all count.** Digits, the decimal mark, backspace and ±step press as the finger lands (pointerdown), once per finger, so two quick taps or two overlapping fingers both register, as on the phone's keyboard; a key shows pressed until the finger lifts. The pointerdown is cancelled, so WebKit drops that touch's click, which the keys no longer need; Enter and Space on a focused key still press it (`padKeyPresses`). The pad has `touch-action: manipulation` and its keys `none`, so a double tap never zooms. Next / Check, Same as last and Target stay on click: they move the pad or check the set, so a resting thumb must not fire them, and the click is the tap iPhone Safari needs before the rest's tone can play.
